THE SOUL BEHIND THE CRAFT

An Ancestral Haven in Nagra Toli

Bridging the comforting depth of Korean street delicacies with the living wisdom of Jharkhand’s indigenous forest traditions.

Mandi Eddpa Heritage

Conceived by entrepreneur Anju Toppo as an artistic extension of tribal culinary resilience and community pride.

Housed within the ancestral Toppo family residence, Cafe De Arte is far more than a typical dining space—it is an intimate cultural conversation.

Founded in November 2023 by Anju Toppo, the cafe draws deep philosophical parallels between Korean food traditions—rich with slow-simmered broths, handmade dumplings, and fermented delights—and the ancestral village recipes of Jharkhand like steamed pitha, earthy finger-millet (madua/ragi), and forest herbs.

The walls celebrate this dual narrative: vintage pop-culture cinema posters of Darth Vader and Popeye converse seamlessly with black-and-white anthropological portraits of Adivasi communities, creating a cozy sanctuary where art, heritage, and flavor meet.

Indigenous Grains

Promoting wholesome finger-millet (Madua/Ragi) dumplings, honoring Jharkhand’s agricultural legacy.

Tribe Tree Corner

A curated artisanal space supporting indigenous weavers, traditional handlooms, and tribal textile designs.
